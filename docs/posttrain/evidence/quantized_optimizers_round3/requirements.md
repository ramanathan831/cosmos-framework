# Requirement-to-evidence map

This map separates implementation correctness, development selection and the
locked final comparison. The first two campaigns remain unchanged. The final
36-run outcome comparison and its six-run corrected product endpoint are complete.
The native arithmetic diagnostic and separately locked K/V precision screen are
complete. All owned allocations are terminal. Prior results are preserved; the
requested product qualification remains open.
